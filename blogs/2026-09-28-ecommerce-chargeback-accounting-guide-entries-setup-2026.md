What happens when an online sale you’ve already recorded gets reversed weeks later? In accounting terms, that’s a chargeback: A forced reversal of a completed sale, initiated by the customer’s bank rather than the merchant. For ecommerce businesses, the disputed transaction and any associated dispute fees should be tracked separately.
